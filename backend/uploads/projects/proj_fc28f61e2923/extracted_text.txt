1. Company snapshot
MagicBook Technologies builds AI-powered notebooks and apps that connect
handwriting to AI. Tagline: "The Cognitive Interface Between Handwriting and Artificial
Intelligence."
Positioning: education first, with stated ambitions in enterprise and creative work. The
long-term vision is a universal cognitive interface across education, science, medicine,
engineering and creative industries.
Founders: Helder Silva (Founder & Head of Product, PhD in Neuroscience, Portuguese
entrepreneur awards) and Adriana Oliveira (Founder & Head of Growth for Latin
America). The company's legal and privacy pages are in Portuguese, which suggests a
Portugal/Brazil base.
Distribution: integrated with paper manufacturers. A MagicBook code is printed on
each notebook page; scanning it links the paper notes to the app.
Users: about 25,000 (internal figure, Oct 2026). Note: the public website claims
"100,000+ users worldwide". Reconcile before simulation and decide which figure to
treat as true.
Feedback to date: positive only. No negative feedback has been recorded. [FILL] any
ratings, NPS, reviews, support themes or churn data.
2. Product portfolio
The website lists these products: MagicBook, Jandia, Wise Book, Estudia, Plania and Dino.
MagicBoard is not listed publicly. [FILL] what MagicBoard is, and what Jandia, Wise Book,
Estudia and Plania each are and who they target.
MagicBook (core)
1. Scan: photograph handwritten or printed notes; the page is detected automatically.
2. Review and extract: OCR converts notes into editable text for correction.

3. Apply intelligence: summarize, translate, solve equations, generate quizzes, create
flashcards, rephrase, shorten, chat with notes.
Other stated capabilities:
Automatic organization into structured tabs, plus a searchable knowledge archive that
grows over time.
AI assistant that answers questions aligned to the school curriculum with verified
sources (a "personalized tutor").
Voice recording of classes, transcription to notes, and text-to-speech playback.
Dino
Turns children's drawings into animated, educational stories (science, history, values,
creativity). Flow: draw on paper, scan with phone, AI animates, personalized educational
story.
3. Current strengths (from available evidence)
Paper-to-digital bridge via printed page codes, which creates a hardware-light
distribution model through notebook partners.
Broad AI feature set built on one OCR/knowledge core.
Curriculum alignment claim and multimodal learning (written, spoken, interactive).
Strong founder credentials; Portuguese-language roots.
4. Known gaps and risks to test
Feature breadth may dilute focus: many AI tools (summarize, translate, chat, quiz,
flashcards, voice) overlap with free general-purpose AI apps.
Dependence on notebook partners for acquisition and printed codes.
Handwriting OCR accuracy across languages, scripts and messy children's
handwriting. [FILL] accuracy figures.
Child data privacy and consent: LGPD (Brazil), GDPR (Portugal/EU), COPPA and FERPA
(USA).
Curriculum alignment depth per country is unverified. [FILL] which curricula are
actually supported.
No public pricing, business model or USA presence found on the website. [FILL]
